## Version B (LinkedIn, longer)

I spent today at the AI Tinkerers "Agents Everywhere" global hackathon building
something I actually wanted to exist.

My inbox never got away from me because triage is hard. It got away from me
because opening the laptop is a decision I kept deferring.

So I built an agent that doesn't wait to be opened. It calls me.

Overnight it sorts the inbox into the same five GTD buckets I've used for years.
In the morning the phone rings: "You got 12 overnight. I filed 8, you don't need
to hear about those. 4 actually need you."

Then it walks me through the 4. I can interrupt it, ask what an email actually
says, and it reads me the sender's words before I decide. When I say "waiting
for," the label moves in Gmail while I'm still on the call.

The part I'm most pleased with is the least technically impressive: it writes
down what I decided, so the next call knows. "You'd been waiting on the contract
review since the 3rd — they've replied, so it's back on you." A chat window
can't say that sentence.

One deliberate constraint: it labels, and only labels. It never sends, never
deletes, never forwards. Anything that reaches another human stays with me.

Built with the @OpenAI Realtime API, @Twilio Voice, and the Gmail API.

First hackathon. Came to learn and meet people, which I'd recommend over trying
to win.
